## Brand & Style

This design system embodies an editorial-meets-counterculture specialty coffee aesthetic: playful retro-modern chic. It deliberately rejects predictable earth-tone cafe tropes, replacing them with a high-fashion, print-magazine sensibility that feels vibrant, witty, and bespoke.

### Core Attributes
- **Artisanal & High-Fashion**: Evoking high-gloss food & wine periodicals, oversized titling, and gallery-worthy art direction.
- **Playfully Retro**: Subtle postal stamps, sticker badges, dynamic rotation accents, and mid-century modern European espresso bar flair.
- **Fresh & Youthful**: Bold color blocking paired with generous, deliberate whitespace and crisp typography.

### Emotional Target
The user experience should feel like stepping into a concept espresso atelier in Copenhagen or Tokyo—design-led, sensory, indulgent, yet effortlessly approachable.

## Colors

The palette balances intense extraction tones with airy freshness. 

### Palette Application
- **Deep Burgundy (`#71352F`)**: The hero accent. Used for primary CTAs, key titles, decorative borders, and brand stamps. Evokes dark cherries, winey acidity, and rich roast profiles.
- **Powder Blue (`#A9D2EE`)**: The expressive secondary. Used for interactive pill highlights, energetic badge fills, secondary buttons, and playful contrast elements.
- **Pastel Blue (`#D9EAF5`)**: The subtle background accent. Used for secondary containers, notification banners, tint layers, and table header stripes.
- **Warm Cream (`#F5EFE6`)**: The global canvas base. Creates an organic, tactile paper-like surface that avoids the sterile glare of pure cool grays.
- **Charcoal Black (`#171719`)**: High-contrast, near-black ink for readable, editorial typography and crisp geometric framing.
- **Pure White (`#FFFFFF`)**: Reserved for cards, modular trays, and pop-out elements to induce crisp dimensionality against the cream background.

## Typography

The type system stages a dialogue between expressive display geometry and clean, geometric sans legibility.

- **Headlines (Syne)**: Characterful, wide, and distinctive. Headlines command immediate attention, mimicking avant-garde editorial publications. Display styles should be tracked slightly tighter (`-0.02em` to `-0.03em`) for punchy cohesion.
- **Body & Labels (Outfit)**: Pure, modernist sans-serif delivering effortless legibility for roast profiles, tasting notes, checkout flows, and operational metadata.
- **Editorial Microcopy**: Labels and badges use uppercase weights with open tracking to emulate classic packaging batch stamps.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy merges an asymmetric magazine spread with a structured 12-column digital grid.

### Grid Rhythm & Responsive Behaviors
- **Desktop (1024px+)**: 12-column fluid grid, 3rem margins, 1.5rem gutters. Product grids feature alternating vertical offsets to mirror physical retail shelves and magazine collages.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px)**: 8-column grid, 2rem margins, 1.25rem gutters.
- **Mobile (<768px)**: 4-column grid, 1.25rem margins, 1rem gutters. Horizontal carousel feeds with trailing peeks are prioritized over compressed multi-column stacks.

### Spacing Principles
- Generous outer breathing room allows Warm Cream to ground high-density typography and saturated product shots.
- Compact internal card spacing maintains snappy, scannable flavor metrics (Origin, Altitude, Notes, Process).

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids synthetic software blurs in favor of tactile paper layers and warm ambient depth.

### Layering Hierarchy
1. **Canvas Layer (`#F5EFE6`)**: Flat background base.
2. **Structural Cards (`#FFFFFF`)**: Lifted slightly using soft, tinted ambient drop shadows:
   - `box-shadow: 0 8px 24px -4px rgba(113, 53, 47, 0.08), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(23, 23, 25, 0.04);`
3. **Interactive Floating Elements / Popovers**:
   - `box-shadow: 0 16px 36px -6px rgba(113, 53, 47, 0.14), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(23, 23, 25, 0.06);`
4. **Retro Stamp & Sticker Badges**: Crisp 1px outline (`#71352F`) or solid fills placed with dynamic rotational offsets (-2deg to +3deg) sitting completely flat or pinned over card edges.

## Shapes

The shape system blends friendly mid-century curvatures with precise geometric bounds.

- **Base Radius (`0.5rem` / `8px`)**: Applied to standard interactive form controls, inputs, and inline indicators.
- **Large Radius (`1rem` / `16px`)**: Product cards, recipe boards, and modal containers.
- **Extra Large Radius (`1.5rem` / `24px`)**: Hero media framing, collection banners, and feature callouts.
- **Full Pill (`9999px`)**: Interactive buttons, category filters, sensory chips, and flavor notes tags.
- **Notched/Scalloped Stamps**: Specialized SVG masks for vintage roast date stamps and origin seals.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Deep Burgundy background (`#71352F`), Pure White text (`#FFFFFF`), full pill radius, `Outfit` label font (semi-bold, uppercase, tracked). Hover state shifts brightness and lifts subtly via ambient shadow.
- **Secondary**: Powder Blue fill (`#A9D2EE`) with Charcoal Black text (`#171719`), pill shape.
- **Ghost / Outline**: 1.5px border in `#71352F` over transparent canvas, Burgundy text.

### Chips & Flavor Tags
- **Tasting Note Pills**: Pure White or Pastel Blue (`#D9EAF5`) backgrounds with 1px border (`#71352F` at 15% opacity), Charcoal Black text, fully rounded.
- **Roast Level Indicators**: Segmented pill bars filling from Pastel Blue to Deep Burgundy.

### Cards (Product & Editorial)
- **Product Card**: Pure White surface, `1rem` rounded corners, subtle warm burgundy ambient shadow. Editorial image flush at top with rounded inner container, bold Syne typography for blend name, clean tabular layout for coffee origins.
- **Interactive State**: Elevates on hover with a slight upward translation (`-4px`) and shadow intensification.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- **Fields**: Pure White background on Warm Cream canvas, 1px subtle border (`rgba(23, 23, 25, 0.15)`), `0.5rem` radius.
- **Focus**: Border turns to solid Deep Burgundy (`#71352F`) with a 3px ring in `#D9EAF5`.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- **Checkboxes**: `0.25rem` radius, 1.5px Charcoal Black border. Active state fills with Deep Burgundy and a crisp white tick mark.
- **Radios**: Concentric circles; active state displays a Deep Burgundy inner pip within a Powder Blue halo.

### Bespoke Accent Components
- **Origin Stamp**: Circular or scalloped badge displaying harvest year and farm coordinate micro-copy, tilted at `3deg`.
- **Flavor Profile Matrix**: Minimalist spider chart or dual-tone pill bar rendered with Deep Burgundy and Powder Blue accents.
